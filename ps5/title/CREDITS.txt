Credits and licences
====================

Free Riders Recompiled for PS5 is a port of another project to the PS5. Most
of the work behind it is theirs. Each licence's full text is in licenses/.

Free Riders Recompiled
----------------------
The game this port runs. Free Riders Recompiled is by YuutaTsubasa (悠太翼):
  https://github.com/YuutaTsubasa/Free-Riders-Recompiled
with code and research by knuckleslee, testing by Saiyokage, and the many
players credited in its README for their reports and logs.
GNU GPL v3 (licenses/GPL-3.0.txt). This port is GPL v3 too.

Free Riders Recompiled credits, and builds on:
  XenonRecomp and XenosRecomp (hedge-dev and contributors; XenosRecomp as
    pinned by sonicnext-dev): the PowerPC and shader recompilers. MIT.
  Unleashed Recompiled (hedge-dev) and Marathon Recompiled (sonicnext-dev):
    the recompilation ports it learned from.
  Xenia (the Xenia project): reference for the Xbox 360's behaviour.
  No Kinect Patch by Rei-SanTH: reference for the Kinect emulation (none of
    its code is used).

The PS5 side
------------
PS5_Vulkan by Mihawk-99, built on ps5-native-app-boilerplate by
  BlackBearReloaded: RADV (Mesa's Vulkan driver) for the PS5, the title
  converter and signer, and the generated libc.prx. GNU GPL v3.
  https://github.com/mihawk-99/PS5_Vulkan
PS5_Mesa by Mihawk-99, on Mesa (Mesa contributors, freedesktop.org, with
  AMD's AddrLib): the graphics driver. MIT and others (licenses/Mesa/).
PS5_PayloadSDK by Mihawk-99, on the ps5-payload-dev SDK by John Törnblom
  (with Aleksei Kulaev): the PS5 toolchain, headers and platform layer.
  GNU GPL v3.
SDL 2 (Sam Lantinga and contributors), with its PS5 port by John Törnblom
  (ps5-payload-dev): controller and audio. zlib licence (licenses/SDL2.txt).

The PS5 port
------------
Ported to the PS5 by HopeZEXAL, with Claude (Anthropic).
This PS5 port was made with AI ("vibe-coded"): nearly all of its code was
written by Claude, Anthropic's AI model. HopeZEXAL directed it: decided what
the port should be and do, tested every build on a real PS5, and reported
what worked and what didn't. (Free Riders Recompiled itself was also
developed with the help of AI models, as its README says.)
The icon and background art are original to this port.
